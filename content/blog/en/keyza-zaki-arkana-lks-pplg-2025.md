On Saturday, 26 April 2025, the MGMP (Subject Teacher Working Group) of Kota Tasikmalaya held its Lomba Kompetensi Siswa (LKS) for the Pengembangan Perangkat Lunak dan Gim (PPLG) program at SMK BPN Tasikmalaya. The event ran in two categories: Web Technology, where participants built a web-based application, and IT Software, where they developed an operating-system-based program.

## The result

In the Web Technology category, Keyza Zaki Arkana — a student of SMKN 2 Kota Tasikmalaya, then in class X PPLG — finished **second with a score of 137.50**.

The full Web Technology ranking:

| Place | Name | Score |
| ----- | ---- | ----- |
| 1 | Ilma Sintia | 139.25 |
| 2 | Keyza Zaki Arkana | 137.50 |
| 3 | Syakib Arsalan | 137.25 |

## How the category was judged

Scoring was not limited to whether the application worked. The judging criteria weighted the function of the program or application as the main component, with design and user experience counted as separate aspects.

## What happened next

Each department in the city is required to hold its own LKS, because every department has an event at the provincial level. Other departments, such as Otomotif and TKJ, had already run theirs earlier in the same week.

The winners at the Kota Tasikmalaya level were brought together into a single contingent to represent the city at the LKS Provinsi Jawa Barat, which was scheduled for May 2025.
